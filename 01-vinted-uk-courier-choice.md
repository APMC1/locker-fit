# Which Vinted UK courier should you enable? InPost vs Evri vs Royal Mail

Buyers pick and pay for postage at checkout. You only choose which couriers appear. Enable the ones you can actually drop off near you, then match the parcel to the courier’s size cap so the locker or shop does not refuse it.

Vinted assigns parcel size from the listing details. After the buyer pays, the size and courier cannot be changed. If the packed item is over a carrier’s limit, they can charge extra or refuse it.

Sources for the limits below: [Vinted parcel size limits](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1227), [how shipping works](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/753-how-shipping-works), [manage shipping options](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/447), [InPost help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953), [Evri help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706), [Royal Mail × Vinted](https://www.royalmail.com/vinted-postage), [Post Office drop-off guide](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/mail/guides/vinted-postage-options).

## Quick pick

| If the packed item is… | Prefer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A folded tee, jeans, thin jumper, small bag | **24/7 InPost Locker / Shop Pick-up** | Fits the locker box. QR code, often no home printer. Many lockers open late. |
| Too bulky for a locker (coat on a hanger shape, shoe box, bundle) | **Evri** | Evri’s girth formula allows awkward shapes better than a rigid locker. QR at ParcelShops. |
| Buyer wants Royal Mail, or you drop at a Post Office every time | **Royal Mail Tracked 48 / 2nd Class Medium Parcel** | Every Post Office takes Royal Mail. Label handling is the fussiest of the three. |
| Heavy / bulky (kids’ furniture, rugs, dinner sets) | **Evri heavy** or **Royal Mail medium parcel** | Standard clothing routes cap at 2 kg. Heavy routes go higher. Check the table. |

Vinted’s own “standard items” examples are clothing, shoes, bags, jewellery, accessories. “Heavy items” examples are consoles, kids’ furniture, pet beds, dinner sets, scooters, rugs.

## Official size and weight caps (standard clothing-type items)

From Vinted Help “Parcel size limits” (standard items, max 2 kg on these routes):

| Provider | Max dimensions | Max weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24/7 InPost Locker \| Shop Pick-up | 64 × 38 × 41 cm | 2 kg |
| InPost Home Delivery | 64 × 38 × 41 cm | 2 kg |
| Evri | L + 2W + 2H ≤ 245 cm | 2 kg |
| Royal Mail 2nd Class Medium Parcel | 61 × 46 × 46 cm | 2 kg |

Heavy-item routes on the same help page:

| Provider | Max dimensions | Max weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evri | L + 2W + 2H ≤ 245 cm | 15 kg |
| Royal Mail 2nd Class Medium Parcel | 61 × 46 × 46 cm | 20 kg |

Measure the **packed** parcel, not the garment on a hanger. Fold or roll first. If the shape is irregular, measure the longest, widest and highest points.

## How drop-off actually works

- **InPost locker / shop pick-up:** In the sale chat tap Get shipping label. You can use a QR code (no home print) or a printable label. Scan the QR at the locker; do not close the door until the locker tells you to. Mainland England / Scotland / Wales. See [InPost shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953).
- **Evri:** Prepaid Vinted label. Home delivery = ParcelShop/locker → buyer’s address. ParcelShop = shop/locker → shop/locker. Tracking can take up to 48 hours to show in the Vinted chat. See [Evri shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706).
- **Royal Mail:** Buyer selects Tracked 48 in Vinted. You get a label link by email and in-app. Drop at a Royal Mail shop, Post Office, locker or customer service point. See [royalmail.com/vinted-postage](https://www.royalmail.com/vinted-postage).
- **Post Office as a hub:** Every branch takes Royal Mail. A growing number also take Evri and/or DPD. Filter the [Post Office branch finder](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/mail/guides/vinted-postage-options) for Evri / DPD before you toggle those on in Vinted.

## Seller settings that matter

Path: Profile → Settings → Postage.

- Turn on only couriers you will actually use this week.
- Some toggles are locked so at least one option always exists.
- More options usually means faster sales. A disabled option can still appear if it is the only way that buyer can check out. If you cannot use it, Vinted says you can cancel without negative feedback.
- Changes apply to **future** orders only. After checkout the courier is fixed. The only change path is cancel and rebuy.
- You cannot switch off all providers, and you cannot switch off international shipping as a category.

Buyer pays postage. Rates depend on parcel size and both postcodes. You do not set the price.

## Checklist before you list

1. Fold the garment the way you will pack it.
2. Measure L × W × H of the packed bundle.
3. Weigh it (kitchen scales are fine for clothes).
4. If L × W × H fits 64 × 38 × 41 cm and weight ≤ 2 kg, InPost locker is usually the least-friction default.
5. If it fails the locker box but passes Evri’s L+2W+2H ≤ 245 cm rule, enable Evri.
6. Photograph the packed parcel (inside and out) before you drop it. Vinted recommends this for disputes.
7. Confirm your nearest locker / ParcelShop / Post Office is open when you actually ship (Vinted expects prompt send; you can ask the buyer for a 3- or 5-business-day extension if needed).

## What this page does not claim

- Exact checkout prices. Those are shown to the buyer in the app and change with size and location. Third-party blogs quote “from £1.99 / £2.49”; treat those as unverified unless you see them in your own checkout.
- Which courier is “always cheapest.” Cheap for a tee can be expensive for a coat.
- Compensation amounts if a parcel is lost. That sits in each carrier’s terms, linked from [Vinted’s carrier T&Cs page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/757-shipping-options-for-buyers).
