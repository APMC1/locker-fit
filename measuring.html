How to measure a garment for a Vinted listing

Pit-to-pit, waist and length are garment measurements. Vinted’s size charts are body measurements, and they are not the same number. This page says which is which, then gives a flat-lay method you can write into the description in your own words.

What Vinted actually publishes

  • Put measurements in the description. Vinted’s listing guidance says a description should include the item’s measurements, plus flaws, alterations and missing labels. It does not say where to put the tape.
  • The buyer is told the figures came from you. The measurements help page says “The seller provided these measurements. Ask them if you need more details.” The labels on that page are shoulder width and length. Pit-to-pit and waist are not defined there.
  • Size charts are body ranges, for reference. The men’s chart lists chest, waist and hip in centimetres and says international sizing is not standardised, the chart may not apply to every brand, and exact measurements help if you are unsure. The women’s jeans chart maps a W number to a UK size and says the item’s exact measurements may vary by brand. Neither chart is a set of points on a flat garment.
  • Collar size is the method Vinted does spell out. For a button-up shirt, use a flexible sewing or tailoring tape around the neck at the Adam’s apple, with enough slack for two fingers. That is a measurement of the person, not of the shirt laid flat.
  • Parcel centimetres are a different job. Length, width and height of the packed box are for the carrier limit. They do not belong in the size line of the listing. The coat page is that check.

Flat-lay method

Vinted does not publish this procedure. Use it so a buyer can repeat the same points, and name the points in the description. Lay the garment flat, front up, on a table. Smooth it. Do not stretch the fabric to hit a size. Use a soft tape, in centimetres. Measure the garment, not the person, except for the collar method above.

  1. Pit to pit. From the seam at the base of one armhole to the seam at the base of the other, straight across the front. Write “pit to pit, garment flat”. Do not double it and call the result the chest size from Vinted’s chart. That chart is a body range. If you also give the doubled figure, write “doubled”.
  2. Waist. For trousers or jeans, across the waistband from side seam to side seam, buttoned or fastened, garment flat, not pulled tight. Write “waistband flat”. A W label on the jeans chart is not this number. If the label says W30 and the flat waistband is 38 cm, report both and do not “correct” one into the other.
  3. Length. For a top, from the highest point of the shoulder seam at the neck, straight down to the hem. Vinted’s label is only the word “Length”, so say where you started. For trousers, from the top of the waistband down the outer leg to the hem, and write “outer leg”.
  4. Shoulder width, when you give it. This is the label Vinted shows. Take it seam to seam across the back of the shoulders, garment flat. It is not the same point as pit to pit. Write “shoulder seam to shoulder seam”.

What to type in the listing

One line is enough. Example for a jumper: “Pit to pit 52 cm, garment flat. Length 68 cm from shoulder seam at the neck to the hem. Label says M.” Example for jeans: “Waistband 39 cm flat, buttoned. Outer leg 78 cm. Label says W30 L32.”

Keep the size field as the label in the garment. Use the description for the tape figures. Buyers are told to ask if they need another point.

Checklist

  1. Read the label. Put that size in the size field.
  2. Lay the garment flat. Do not stretch it.
  3. Pit to pit, or waistband flat, then length. Note the start and end of each one.
  4. If you measured a person (collar), say so. Do not mix that number with a flat garment number under one label.
  5. Do not copy the packed-parcel measurements into the description as if they were the garment.

What I use to measure

  • Measuring tape (cm) — soft, the sort used for sewing. A steel rule is a poor fit around a collar and a poor fit along a curved hem.

What this page does not claim

  • That pit-to-pit or a flat waistband is Vinted’s official definition. Those words are not in the help pages linked above.
  • That a flat measurement converts to a UK letter size. The charts say they may not apply to every brand.
  • A body measurement you have not taken. Collar size is the exception, and only when you follow the neck method on the shirt chart.
