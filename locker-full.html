What to do when the InPost locker is full

A full locker and a parcel that does not fit are different problems. InPost tells you to leave a full locker and come back or use another one. It tells you not to force a parcel into a compartment that is too small.

This page has no product links. The directory’s other pages do. Disclosure.

Sources: InPost: the locker is full, InPost Help (availability and how often lockers are emptied), InPost: the locker is too small, InPost contact, Vinted parcel size limits, Can I change the parcel size at the drop-off point?.

The locker is full

  • Try again later, or the next day. InPost says every locker is emptied at least once a day, usually in the morning.
  • Use a different locker nearby.
  • Check before you travel. The InPost app shows how many small, medium and large compartments are free, and it suggests another locker if that one is full. The website can show that a locker has space. InPost says compartment sizes are not shown on the website.
  • A missing locker on the map can mean it is out of order. InPost says that is usually fixed in a day or two, sometimes longer.

Vinted does not publish a separate “locker full” procedure. If a compartment will not open or close, Vinted says to contact the carrier. InPost’s contact page says that if you are at the locker and need help, call 0330 335 0950.

The compartment is too small

Do not force the parcel in. InPost says to close the door without putting the parcel inside, then tap My parcel doesn’t fit on the screen. For a Vinted parcel it says you can then choose a different compartment size on the screen.

InPost’s compartment sizes, from that same page:

  • Extra small: 4 × 23 × 40 cm
  • Small: 8 × 38 × 64 cm
  • Medium: 19 × 38 × 64 cm
  • Large: 41 × 38 × 64 cm

Vinted’s own cap for an InPost locker or shop on a standard item is still 64 × 38 × 41 cm and 2 kg. A larger compartment on the screen does not raise that cap. If the packed parcel is over the large compartment, it is not a locker parcel. The coat page is that decision.

Vinted also says that at a locker you follow the on-screen steps to select another locker size, and you do not close the door until the screen asks you to. At a shop, you hand the parcel to the staff.

Checklist

  1. Before you leave, open the InPost app and check compartment sizes at that locker.
  2. If it is full, pick another locker or wait for the daily empty. Do not leave the parcel beside the locker.
  3. If the door opens and the parcel will not go in, take it out, close the door, and use “My parcel doesn’t fit”.
  4. If nothing large enough is free and the parcel is within 64 × 38 × 41 cm, try another locker the app shows as having a large compartment.
  5. If the parcel itself is over that size or over 2 kg, stop. It needs a different carrier, not a second visit.

What this page does not claim

  • That Vinted will extend the shipping deadline because a locker was full. The help pages linked here do not say that.
  • That a shop is always open when the locker is full. Check the shop’s hours in the app.
  • A product that frees a compartment. Nothing in this checklist is something you buy.
