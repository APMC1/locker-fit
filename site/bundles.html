Bundles and how the parcel size jumps

A bundle is several listings from you, bought as one order, and sent as one parcel. The size Vinted picks is not a promise that the packed parcel still fits an InPost locker. Weigh and measure it after it is packed.

What a bundle is allowed to be

  • Up to 100 items from the same seller, in one order. After purchase, each listing is marked sold.
  • Heavy or bulky items are bought one at a time, not added to a bundle. The same is true of items bought with Electronics Verification or Item Verification.
  • An international bundle is capped by item price: £120 from the US and £110 from Australia, including bundle discounts and not the postage. That cap is not a UK weight limit.
  • Everything goes in one parcel. Vinted’s packing rules say each item is wrapped and not moving around. A parcel can hold at most two electronic devices with batteries.

Where the size comes from

Vinted selects a parcel size from the listing details, such as category, size and brand. You measure the item and the packaging before you accept that suggestion. The buyer pays a price based on that size. Once they have paid, Vinted says the carrier, the label type, the address and the parcel size cannot be changed, except by cancelling the order.

Bundles have a drop-off exception on a separate help page. If you are at the shop or locker and the autoselected size is wrong, Vinted says you can change the parcel size there at no extra cost:

  • Shop: hand the parcel over. The carrier changes the size on their side. Stay inside that carrier’s maximum.
  • Locker: follow the screen to select another locker size. Do not close the door until it tells you to.

If the parcel was bigger than the autoselected size and you were charged extra, that page says to contact Vinted with the receipt so they can compensate the charge. It does not say this for a size you chose wrongly on an individual item. The Royal Mail note on the size-limits page is the other way round: compensation is for a size Vinted selected, not one you selected yourself.

The 2 kg jump

Standard clothing on the size-limits page, including an InPost locker, InPost home and the Royal Mail medium parcel, is capped at 2 kg. Evri’s standard row is also 2 kg. The heavy rows are Evri at 15 kg and Royal Mail at 20 kg. InPost is not on the heavy table.

Two jumpers can still be a locker parcel. A coat plus jeans often is not, because the packed weight or the packed depth has gone past 64 × 38 × 41 cm and 2 kg. Weigh the sealed parcel, not the items on their hangers. If it is over 2 kg, do not enable an InPost option for that bundle and hope the locker will sort it out. Enable Evri or Royal Mail before the buyer pays, using the courier page.

Checklist

  1. Keep heavy, bulky and verification items out of the bundle.
  2. Put every item in one parcel. Wrap them so they cannot slide.
  3. Weigh the sealed parcel. Under 2 kg and within 64 × 38 × 41 cm can still be InPost.
  4. Over 2 kg: Evri or Royal Mail, decided before checkout.
  5. At the drop-off, if the autoselected bundle size is wrong, use the shop or the locker screen. Keep the receipt if you are charged extra.

What I use to pack a bundle

  • Digital kitchen scales — the packed parcel, not the heap of clothes.
  • Poly mailers — a thin two-item bundle. Not an unpadded bag with loose items, which Vinted’s packing examples mark as a bad pack.
  • Packing tape — seams, and the label edges once the label exists.
  • Cardboard boxes (DavPack) — when the bundle is too thick or too structured for a mailer.

What this page does not claim

  • A postage price for a bundle. The buyer sees that at checkout. It is not published as a fixed list.
  • That the drop-off will always accept a parcel over the carrier maximum if you ask. The exception is a wrong autoselected size, inside that carrier’s limit.
  • That Parcel2Go is the next step. Integrated labels still cover a heavy bundle. An off-platform label is a different page, and only after that programme has approved this site.
