# How to pack a coat so it still fits an InPost locker

An InPost locker box on Vinted UK is 64 × 38 × 41 cm, 2 kg, standard-item routes. A coat on a hanger does not fit that. Folded flat and compressed, most coats do. This page is the folding and packing method, not a courier comparison — see [which courier to enable](01-vinted-uk-courier-choice.md) for that.

Sources for the limits: [Vinted parcel size limits](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1227), [choosing the right parcel size](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/51), [InPost shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953).

## Will it fit at all?

Two separate checks, both against the **packed** parcel, not the coat on a hanger:

1. **Weight.** Standard-item routes cap at 2 kg. A wool coat, a padded jacket, or anything with a liner can be close to or over this on its own. Weigh the coat alone before you fold anything — if it is already near 2 kg, stop and plan for Evri or Royal Mail medium parcel instead (see the courier page's heavy-item table).
2. **Dimensions.** 64 × 38 × 41 cm is folded-flat territory, not rolled-into-a-ball territory. A stiff structured coat (tailored wool, a blazer-cut mac) resists folding small and may simply not go under 41 cm in its thickest dimension. A softer shell, puffer or knit-lined coat compresses more easily.

If either check fails, that is the answer: don't force it into a locker parcel. Enable Evri (girth formula allows an awkward shape better than a rigid box) or Royal Mail medium parcel, and say so in the listing if buyers are choosing postage based on locker availability.

## Folding method

1. **Lay the coat flat, front up, buttons or zip done up.** This keeps the shape square instead of floppy.
2. **Fold the sleeves in across the body**, so they lie flat against the torso rather than hanging past the hem.
3. **Fold in thirds lengthwise** (collar to hem) rather than in half. One fold in half on a long coat usually still exceeds 41 cm; two folds gets most knee-length coats under it.
4. **Fold again if needed** to bring the final width under 38 cm and the final height under 41 cm. Check with a tape measure after each fold, not by eye — see the courier page's measuring note.
5. **Press out air, don't stuff.** Padded or down coats compress a surprising amount with hand pressure; a vacuum bag is not necessary for a single coat but can help for a bundle of several.

## Packaging choice

- **Poly mailer:** fine for a thin shell or light raincoat. Vinted's packing guidance wants padding for anything that could be crushed or scuffed in transit — a bare poly mailer with nothing inside but the coat is acceptable for most coats, since the coat itself is the padding, but add a layer of paper or bubble wrap around buttons, zips or embellishments so they don't press through the mailer and mark the fabric.
- **Padded envelope:** better for a coat with hardware (buttons, a belt buckle, metal zip pulls) that could dent through a plain poly mailer.
- **Box:** use a box instead of a mailer if the coat is structured enough that folding it to the target size would crease it badly (tailoring, leather, anything the buyer would reasonably expect uncreased). A box also makes it obvious to the courier that there's a rigid shape inside, which can mean gentler handling in transit — this is a practical observation, not a guarantee from any carrier.

## Checklist

1. Weigh the coat alone. If it's already close to 2 kg, don't bother folding for a locker — use Evri or Royal Mail instead.
2. Fold in thirds lengthwise, sleeves in, buttons/zip done up.
3. Measure the folded, bagged parcel: L × W × H against 64 × 38 × 41 cm.
4. Protect hardware (buttons, buckles, zip pulls) with a layer of paper or bubble wrap if using a poly mailer.
5. Photograph the packed parcel before you drop it off.
6. If it doesn't fit after two folds, stop forcing it — switch courier, don't switch to a bigger "locker" that doesn't exist.

## What this page does not claim

- That every coat fits a locker box. Many genuinely don't, and that's fine — Evri and Royal Mail exist for exactly this case.
- A specific fold count that works for every coat. Fabric stiffness and lining vary; the thirds-fold is a starting point, not a guarantee.
- Carrier handling behaviour (gentler with boxes vs mailers). That's a packing judgement call, not a carrier policy.

## What I use to pack

- Poly mailers or padded envelopes — see [the courier page's kit list](01-vinted-uk-courier-choice.md) for the same links.
- A tape measure (cm), checked after folding, not before.
- Kitchen scales, for the coat alone before you decide which courier to enable.
