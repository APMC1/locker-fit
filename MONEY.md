# How this directory makes money

The pages are inventory. Money starts when a reader who needed tape, scales or a box clicks a tracked link, or buys something you actually sell on Vinted.

There is no measured revenue yet. The £217 “month three” figure from promo riffs on the Lummox essay is an anecdote, not a forecast.

## Honest paths, in order

1. **Affiliates on kit sellers already buy** — kitchen scales, tape, poly mailers (Amazon Associates UK, tag `lockerfit-21`, 24-hour click cookie); bulk boxes (DavPack on Awin, advertiser 29981, from 2%, 30-day cookie); occasional non-Vinted labels (Parcel2Go on Awin, advertiser 18733, 10% new / 1% existing, 30-day cookie). Disclose every time.
2. **Your own Vinted shop** — a packing page that ranks can point at a real listing or bundle. Empty “shop our picks” is just another affiliate block.
3. **A paid one-pager later** — size card, locker-fit sheet. Not a reprint of Vinted Help. Wait until there are 15–30 useful pages.
4. **Display ads last** — after Search Console shows real queries.

## Rules that stay

- Do not invent traffic or earnings.
- Do not put a home address or personal email on the imprint.
- Do not buy through your own affiliate links.
- Do not email a merchant with a story that differs from the live pages.
- Re-check [Vinted parcel size limits](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1227) before publishing a page that quotes them.

## Scaling

Do not run an unattended publisher yet. Six pages are live and there are no measured sales. Each page is one query, the help URLs fetched that day, a flat statement of what those pages do and do not say, and a stop before any number the source did not publish. A nightly job can draft the next page from that shape, then stop. A person checks the official source and commits only the passing draft to the public repository.
