# How this directory makes money

The pages are inventory. Money starts when a URL is public and a reader who needed tape, scales or a box clicks a tracked link, or buys something you actually sell on Vinted.

There is no measured revenue yet. The £217 “month three” figure from promo riffs on the Lummox essay is an anecdote, not a forecast.

## Honest paths, in order

1. **Affiliates on kit sellers already buy** — kitchen scales, tape, poly mailers (Amazon Associates UK); bulk boxes (DavPack on Awin, from 2%, 30-day cookie); occasional non-Vinted labels (Parcel2Go on Awin, 10% new / 1% existing). Amazon’s click cookie is 24 hours. Disclose every time.
2. **Your own Vinted shop** — a packing page that ranks can point at a real listing or bundle. Empty “shop our picks” is just another affiliate block.
3. **A paid one-pager later** — size card, locker-fit sheet. Not a reprint of Vinted Help. Wait until there are 15–30 useful pages.
4. **Display ads last** — after Search Console shows real queries.
