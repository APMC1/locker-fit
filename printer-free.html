Printer-free drop-off: when the QR code is enough

You do not buy a label printer to sell clothes on Vinted. For the carriers this site covers, the label screen offers a QR code. Someone still has to put a label on the parcel. That someone is you, or the shop.

Read the label screen for that order

After the sale, press Get shipping label in the conversation. Vinted says the carrier, the label type (printable or digital), the address and the parcel size cannot be changed afterwards. Cancelling and repeating the order is the way it gives to change them. Use the Vinted-generated label. A label from somewhere else can cancel the order.

What each carrier’s help page says

  • InPost locker or shop. Vinted lets you pick a printable label or a digital QR. If you pick the QR, Vinted says you do not print it at home, at the locker, or at the shop. You scan it to open a compartment, or you show it to the shop. InPost’s own help says the same for Vinted parcels going to lockers or shops: QR or a printable label.
  • InPost home delivery. InPost’s help says a Vinted parcel sent to a home address needs a printed label. Vinted’s InPost article describes the printable-or-QR choice in the seller steps and does not repeat the home-delivery exception in those lines. If the two disagree on your order, follow the label screen in that conversation.
  • Evri. Printable label at home, or a digital QR so the label is printed at the drop-off. Vinted says most ParcelShops have a Print In Store device, and tells you to check that shop before you go. The QR does not stick itself to the parcel.
  • Royal Mail. You get a QR code in the app and by email. Scan it at the drop-off, or show it to the staff, and they print the label. You stick that label on. Do not put the parcel in a postbox, including a postbox drop with a printed QR taped to the parcel. Vinted says Royal Mail will not register the collection, you will not get tracking as proof of shipping, the parcel will not be delivered, and no compensation will be offered.

Other carriers are on their own Vinted articles. This page does not guess their label rules.

If you cannot print

  1. On the label screen, choose the digital label or QR when that order offers it.
  2. Check the drop-off can print before you travel. Evri tells you to. A locker QR for InPost does not need a printer at the locker.
  3. If the only label is a home print and you cannot print it, Vinted’s own fallback is a friend, a library, or another printer. If you still cannot get a label, cancel the order. Do not send the parcel with no Vinted label.

Checklist

  1. Open Get shipping label. Note printable or digital before you leave.
  2. Pack first. A QR does not replace padding or tape.
  3. InPost locker: scan the QR. Do not print it.
  4. Evri or Royal Mail digital: the shop prints. You attach that label, flat, barcode visible, edges taped.
  5. Royal Mail: parcel shop, Post Office, locker or customer service point. Not a postbox.

What I use

No label printer. The thing the packing rules still ask for is tape, including over the edges of a label the shop has printed.

  • Packing tape — seal the parcel and the label edges.

What this page does not claim

  • That every carrier on Vinted is QR-only. Only the pages linked above were read.
  • That a QR means the parcel never gets a paper label. Evri and Royal Mail print one at the drop-off.
  • A winner where Vinted and InPost describe home delivery differently. The order screen is the instruction for that sale.
