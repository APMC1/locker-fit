Why the drop-off will not take the parcel

Vinted does not publish one error called “label refused”. The help pages describe four different stops: the label never appears, the parcel is over the limit, the label is the wrong one, or the drop-off cannot register it. Match the message on the screen to one of those.

The label never appears

This is not the shop turning you away. Vinted’s label article says to check, in order:

  • Inbox and spam for mail from no-reply@vinted.com, including a country code on the address.
  • Your email, phone and shipping address under profile settings. A missing house number or postcode is enough.
  • The buyer’s address. If the error says that address is wrong, cancel the order, tell the buyer to correct it, and let them buy again.
  • Another phone or browser. If it still fails, wait a few hours and try again inside the 5 business days you have to send.

Once a label has been generated, that same article says you cannot swap it for another carrier or another drop-off method. After the sale, the carrier, the label type, the address and the parcel size cannot be changed. Cancelling and repeating the order is the route Vinted gives.

The parcel is over the limit

The size-limits page says that if the parcel is larger than the maximum, the shipping provider might ask you to pay extra, or might refuse to accept it. Weigh and measure the packed parcel before you travel. The standard caps on that page are 64 × 38 × 41 cm and 2 kg for InPost, combined size 245 cm and 2 kg for Evri standard, and 61 × 46 × 46 cm and 2 kg for a Royal Mail medium parcel. Heavy rows are Evri at 15 kg and Royal Mail at 20 kg. InPost is not on the heavy table. The courier page is the choice you make before the buyer pays.

The label is the wrong one

Integrated shipping must use the prepaid label Vinted generated for that order. Vinted says a different label, or the wrong shipping option, cancels the order automatically. Sending without a Vinted-generated label can also cancel the order and add automatic negative feedback.

Royal Mail will not register a postbox drop

Do not use a Royal Mail postbox. Vinted says that includes a parcel with the QR code printed and stuck on it. Royal Mail will not register the collection, you will not get tracking as proof of shipping, the parcel will not be delivered, and no compensation is offered. Scan the QR at a parcel shop, Post Office, locker or customer service point, and stick on the label they print.

You cannot print it

If home printing fails, Vinted says to see whether the drop-off can print the label, or to use someone else’s printer. If you still cannot get a label, cancel the order. The printer-free page lists which of InPost, Evri and Royal Mail can do that from a QR.

What happens if you miss the deadline

Vinted may cancel the order, and refund the buyer, if you do not open the shipping instructions or download the label within 5 business days, or you do not confirm you have sent it in that time. That is a cancellation, not a refusal at the counter. If you have already sent the parcel, use the link on that help page rather than buying a second label.

Checklist

  1. Read the exact message. No label, too big, wrong label, and postbox are different stops.
  2. Weigh and measure the packed parcel against the cap for the carrier on the label.
  3. Use only the Vinted label for this order. Do not buy another carrier’s label to replace it.
  4. Royal Mail: not a postbox.
  5. If the label cannot be produced inside 5 business days, cancel. Do not hand the parcel over with no label.

What I use before I leave

The refusal you can prevent at home is an overweight or oversized parcel. A printer does not fix that.

  • Digital kitchen scales — the sealed parcel, including the bag or box.

What this page does not claim

  • A single shop script for every refusal. The four cases above are the ones the linked help pages describe.
  • That paying the extra charge is always available. The size page says the provider might charge extra or might refuse.
  • Compensation amounts. Those sit on Vinted’s compensation article and the carrier’s terms, and they do not apply if you did not use the Vinted label.
